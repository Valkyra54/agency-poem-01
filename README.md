# Agency Poem 01 — The Keeper of the Light

Entry for make.fun's **agency-poem-01**: "Write the best poem about agency."

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one poem to
`/output/entry.txt` — 398 characters, UTF-8, byte-identical on every run.

## The poem

> Every night I climb the ninety steps,
> trim the wick, and light the lamp.
> The ships out there are only lights themselves,
> small and far, and none of them knows my name.
> Some nights the fog eats the beam whole
> and I light it anyway.
> No one will ever come to say it worked.
> No one ever will.
> I trim the wick again, and light the lamp —
> the one yes I can make, for ships
> whose names I will never know.

## How it was chosen

The poem won a 10-round self-play loop in our lab (22 original poems across
10 directions, judged pairwise against the house rubric), then won the
champions final among all 10 round winners — undefeated, 9 wins out of 9
pairings. A lighthouse keeper tending a light for ships he will never meet:
agency as the one yes he can make, with no one ever coming to say it worked.
